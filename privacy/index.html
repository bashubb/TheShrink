Privacy Policy

Who We Are

This privacy policy covers Shrink: Photo Compressor, which is operated by bashubb, together with the related website or services described here. It covers the app on iOS.

What Data We Collect

Information users provide directly may include:

  • Purchase or subscription information used to unlock or manage paid features

Some data leaves the device to support app functionality such as accounts, syncing, purchases, support, or other online features.

How We Use Data

We use your data in a variety of ways, including:

  • To understand product usage and improve the app.
  • To manage billing-related access and entitlements.

The following providers are used:

  • Analytics: RevenueCat. Used for purchase and subscription analytics, customer history, charts, experiments, and entitlement management.
  • Backend: RevenueCat. Used for purchase validation, subscription management, and entitlement management.
  • Payments: Apple App Store. Used for processing in-app purchases through StoreKit.
  • Website Hosting: GitHub Pages. Used for hosting the Shrink marketing website and privacy policy.

Additional feature details:

  • Photos / Photo Picker: Photos are processed locally on the device and are not uploaded to any server or shared with third parties.
  • StoreKit: In-app purchases are processed by Apple using StoreKit. Purchase and subscription information is shared with RevenueCat to validate purchases and manage entitlements.

When Data is Shared

When an app feature needs online processing, syncing, account handling, or support, relevant data may be sent off the device to operate that feature.

Some data may be shared with outside recipients such as RevenueCat and Inc when that is needed to operate the app’s features.

The following providers are used:

  • Analytics: RevenueCat. Used for purchase and subscription analytics, customer history, charts, experiments, and entitlement management. Privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Backend: RevenueCat. Used for purchase validation, subscription management, and entitlement management. Privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Payments: Apple App Store. Used for processing in-app purchases through StoreKit. Optional for users. Privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Purchase and subscription information is shared with RevenueCat to validate purchases, manage entitlements, and provide subscription analytics. Photos processed by Shrink are not shared with RevenueCat.
